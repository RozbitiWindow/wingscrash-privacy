Wings Crash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Wings Crash ("the game") is developed by Broken Games Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

The game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal contact details.

When you first open the game you are shown a notice. By tapping OK you agree that we collect anonymous gameplay data through Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • Gameplay events, such as which map you start and your score when a run ends.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • An anonymous app instance identifier generated by Firebase.
  • Approximate location (country and city), derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Age range and gender in aggregated form, which Google may provide if you are signed in to a Google account and allow personalised features.

Ads

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data may be used to show ads that are more relevant to you.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law, such as in the EU/EEA, ads are shown according to your consent choices.

How we use it

We use the analytics data only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. We do not sell your data. Data collected by AdMob is used by Google to provide and measure ads.

Third parties

Data is processed by Google on our behalf or under its own terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics): Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob (ads): How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

See also Google's Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, scores, unlocked items and settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.

Retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Your choices and rights

You can stop the data collection at any time by uninstalling the game, and you can control ad personalisation in your device settings. You may ask us to access or delete data related to you by contacting us. If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the rights granted by the GDPR, including the rights of access, deletion and objection.

Children

The game contains no violence and is suitable for all ages (PEGI 3). It contains ads, but no in-app purchases at the moment. We do not ask anyone, including children, for a name, email address or any other personal contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about the data collected, please contact us and we will delete the related data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
