Wings Crash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Wings Crash ("the game") is developed by Broken Games Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. How to delete your account and data.

Information we collect

You can play the game without an account. We never ask for your real name, email address or any other personal contact details.

1. Anonymous analytics (all players)

When you first open the game you are shown a notice. By tapping OK you agree that we collect anonymous gameplay data through Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • Gameplay events, such as which map you start and your score when a run ends.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • An anonymous app instance identifier generated by Firebase.
  • Approximate location (country and city), derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Age range and gender in aggregated form, which Google may provide if you are signed in to a Google account and allow personalised features.

2. Optional account and online leaderboards

If you choose to create an account (Settings → Account settings), we store on our server:

  • The username you choose (3–16 letters, digits or underscore). Please do not use your real name or any personal information.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted one-way hash (bcrypt). We cannot read it.
  • A numeric player ID that we assign to your account.
  • Your country, derived from your IP address when you create the account or sign in, using a local IP-to-country database. We store only the two-letter country code. We do not store your IP address in your account data. The IP address is processed temporarily to answer your request and to protect the service from abuse (for example by limiting the number of requests).
  • Your game progress: your best score on each map, the total amount of coins and puzzles you have earned, and the number of skins you have unlocked. We use these values to calculate your leaderboard ranking.

What other players can see: the leaderboards are public. Other players can see your username, player ID, country flag and scores. Nothing else from your account is shown.

Data is sent to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Ads

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data may be used to show ads that are more relevant to you.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law, such as in the EU/EEA, ads are shown according to your consent choices.

How we use it

We use analytics data only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. We use account data only to run your account and the online leaderboards. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising profiles. Data collected by AdMob is used by Google to provide and measure ads.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent for analytics and personalised ads; performance of the service you asked for (your account and the leaderboards); and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Third parties

Data is processed by Google on our behalf or under its own terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics): Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob (ads): How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

See also Google's Privacy Policy.

Account data is stored on a server operated by us and is not shared with advertisers or other third parties. The IP-to-country lookup uses a database stored on our own server, so your IP address is not sent to any third party for it. This database is provided by DB-IP.com under the CC BY 4.0 licence. Country flag images come from the open-source famfamfam Flag Icons set.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, scores, unlocked items and settings) is stored locally on your device. If you do not create an account, none of it is sent to us. If you create an account, the values listed in section 2 above are also sent to our server.

Retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Account data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, all data connected to it (username, password hash, player ID, country, scores and progress values) is permanently removed from our server immediately.

Delete your account and data

In the game: open Settings → Account settings → Delete account, enter your password and confirm. Your account and all data stored on our server are deleted immediately and this cannot be undone. Your progress on your own device is not affected; to remove it, uninstall the game or clear its data.

If you cannot sign in (for example you forgot your password or no longer have the game installed): email rozbitiokno@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Wings Crash account" and tell us your username and player ID. We may ask you for details that only the owner of the account can know. We will delete the account within 30 days. Because accounts have no email address, passwords cannot be reset; deleting the account lets you create a new one.

To delete analytics data collected through Firebase, contact us as well and include the details we need to find it, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Your choices and rights

You can stop the data collection at any time by uninstalling the game, and you can control ad personalisation in your device settings. You may ask us to access, correct or delete data related to you by contacting us. If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the rights granted by the GDPR, including the rights of access, rectification, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game contains no violence and is suitable for all ages (PEGI 3). It contains ads, but no in-app purchases at the moment. Creating an account is optional. We do not ask anyone, including children, for a real name, email address or any other personal contact details, but the username is public, so we ask players not to use their real name. The leaderboards have no chat or messaging. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about the data collected, please contact us and we will delete the related data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
